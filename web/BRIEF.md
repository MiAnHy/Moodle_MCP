## 1. The Eight Foundational Decisions

1. **Vibe in Three to Five Words**:
   *Technical Precision, Cybernetic Academic, Autonomous Depth.*
   References:
   - *MIT Media Lab Telemetry & Research Consoles*
   - *Linear.app desktop mechanics & high-contrast dark density*
   - *Bloomberg Terminal meets modern academic LMS*

2. **The Visitor Journey**:
   - **Act 1: Recognition** — The modern academic crisis: overloaded educators, delayed grading cycles, administrative drag.
   - **Act 2: Tension** — The peril of naive AI: unchecked autonomous writes, hallucinated grades, prompt-injection vulnerabilities.
   - **Act 3: Turn** — The Universal Moodle MCP Protocol: turning Moodle into a typed, role-scoped, secure model-driven operating system.
   - **Act 4: Substance (The Peak)** — The Two-Phase Mutation Guard: watching an autonomous write safely lock at `PENDING_HUMAN_CONFIRMATION` before human verification.
   - **Act 5: Range** — The Four Bundled Profiles: Admin Ops, Course Designer, Grading Assistant, and Student Tutor.
   - **Act 6: Commitment** — The Dual-Transport Inspector: live switching between Stdio and SSE with one-click terminal connection copy.

3. **Energy Curve**:
   - *Act 1*: Controlled, brooding, analytical (calm).
   - *Act 2*: Tense, cautionary, rising urgency.
   - *Act 3*: Swift, illuminating, structural clarity.
   - *Act 4 (The Peak)*: High intensity, pinpoint focus, tactile relief.
   - *Act 5*: Expansive, architectural, rhythmic exploration.
   - *Act 6*: Decisive, quiet, terminal readiness (action).

4. **Feeling Curve & The Peak**:
   - *Act 1*: **Recognition** — Seeing the exact administrative overhead that slows academic institutions.
   - *Act 2*: **Vulnerability** — Understanding why raw LLMs cannot be trusted with write access to student records.
   - *Act 3*: **Clarity** — Discovering the MCP abstraction layer translating intent into typed Moodle Core calls.
   - *Act 4 (The Peak)*: **Trust & Astonishment** — Seeing an autonomous grading action get caught by the Two-Phase Mutation Latch, demanding human confirmation before writing to PostgreSQL.
   - *Act 5*: **Empowerment** — Realizing every campus role (student, educator, administrator) has a tailored, hardened toolset.
   - *Act 6*: **Readiness** — Putting a cursor into the terminal connection command to deploy the server locally or in the cloud.

   **The Peak**:
   > "It's the site where the autonomous AI tries to submit a grade, and the system literally freezes the wire until you confirm the diff." (Lives in Act 4).

   **The Tell-Someone Sentence**:
   > "It's the site where you can scrub through live MCP protocol packets and watch an AI safely teach and grade in Moodle without breaking institutional integrity."

5. **Signature Move**:
   **The Interactive Dual-Transport Protocol Inspector**:
   A live interactive cockpit element that lets the visitor toggle between **Stdio** (local Claude Desktop / Cursor) and **SSE** (multi-tenant cloud), scrubbing through real JSON-RPC frames, SSRF validation checks, semantic pruner HTML-to-Markdown diffs, and untrusted data tags.

6. **Aesthetic Range**:
   *Dense, High-Contrast Technical Premium-Minimal*.
   - Ultra-dark institutional navy canvas (`#080C14`)
   - Crisp slate and porcelain typography (`#F1F5F9`, `#94A3B8`)
   - Electric cobalt and cyan protocol accents (`#2563EB`, `#38BDF8`)
   - Monospace telemetry badges and crisp 1px borders (`#1E293B`)

7. **Grammar**:
   **Instrument / Cockpit Grammar** (blended with dimensional scroll staging).
   Telemetry bars, live status badges, verified terminal output, and interactive protocol controls rather than passive marketing cards.

8. **Assets**:
   Procedural SVGs, crisp technical blueprints, real JSON payloads from the test suite fixtures, and interactive CSS/JS components. Zero stock photography, zero generic clay dioramas.
